The poet

More than eight hundred Hindi poems, written across thirty-five years. A published collection. And fifty songs that came out of them.

I'm Dr Abhishek Srivastava — a scientist by profession, a poet by soul.

I have been writing Hindi poetry for more than thirty-five years, and sharing it publicly for over fifteen — poem by poem, on social media, long before any of it was ever sung. There are more than eight hundred verses, poems and shayaris now.

A hundred of them became a published collection, Dil Ke Sau Rang. In 2025 they started becoming songs. I write every word myself and direct how each one sounds; the technology is an instrument in the room, not the author. How these songs are made →

Let the words sing. Let the melodies speak.

— Abhishek Srivastava

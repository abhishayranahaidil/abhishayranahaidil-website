The poet

परिचय

I'm Dr Abhishek Srivastava — a scientist by profession, a poet by soul.

I have been writing Hindi poetry for more than thirty-five years, long before any of it was ever sung. In 2025 the poems started becoming songs. I write every word myself and direct how each one sounds; the technology is an instrument in the room, not the author.

A song here is a memory, a mood, or an argument I was having with myself. Many began as pages in दिल के सौ रंग — a hundred poems, published before a note of this existed. How these songs are made →

Let the words sing. Let the melodies speak.

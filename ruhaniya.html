About

Love that behaves like faith

Five songs approaching one feeling from five directions — longing, devotion, the promise, the first sight, and the silence afterwards.

Every word on this album was published as poetry before it was ever sung. The lyrics come straight from a printed collection; only the vocal and the music are AI-assisted, and every take was rejected until the tune matched the poem rather than the other way round. We have not found another Hindi album that can say the same. How it was made →

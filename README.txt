GOING LIVE
1. Upload everything here to your web root, keeping /assets as a folder.
   Include the hidden .htaccess file.
2. Point your domain at it and disconnect Gamma.
Old links such as /mere-vatan keep working.

DOMAINS — still to decide
You own abhishayrana.com AND abhishayranahaidil.com, and both currently
serve the same site. Pick one as primary and 301-redirect the other, or
search engines split your ranking between them. Tell Claude which you
choose and the canonical tags get updated across every page.

ADDING A SONG — no help needed
1. Open add-song.html.
2. Fill the form, press "Build the files".
3. Paste the one-line block into songs-data.js after  window.SONGS = [
4. Download the song page it gives you and upload it.
Everything else updates itself.

The words

Written by me, by hand — more than eight hundred poems over thirty-five years, shared publicly for over fifteen of them. Many were published as poems in a printed collection before they became songs, which means the writing is dated and traceable. The lyrics are original and unencumbered — no samples, no adaptations, no third-party claims.

The recordings use AI-assisted vocals and instrumentation under my direction. The writing does not. The full statement is here.
